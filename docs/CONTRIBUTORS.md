# Contributors

Devpost asks every team to say who did what. This file is a template: **each member fills in their own
section, in their own words, and only with things they did or can vouch for.** Nobody else (and no tool)
should write another person's section. Replace every `TBD`. Delete this paragraph when all sections are done.

Notes for everyone:

- **Git history is not a record of contribution.** Most commits in the current history carry the AI
  assistant's author name (`git shortlog -sne` shows it), because the assistant committed on the team's
  behalf. Describe what you did, not what the log says.
- **AI assistance was used.** Parts of the code, tests and documentation were produced with AI assistance
  and reviewed by the team. In your section, say honestly what you wrote yourself, what you
  directed or reviewed, and what you only read. The hackathon rules may require a specific statement; the
  team should check the wording (the rules page was not accessible when this file was written).
- Use real full names as they appear on Devpost.
